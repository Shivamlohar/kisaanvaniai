Ground Reality

The Three Barriers Facing Indian Smallholders

83% of Indian agriculturalists operate on marginal landholdings and remain excluded from modern text-first apps.

1. Zero / Low Functional Literacy

Millions of smallholders cannot spell pathogen names or read text-heavy guidelines. When apps require typing in formal English or Standard Hindi, adoption collapses.

2. Dialect & Linguistic Diversity

Over 720 dialects are spoken across rural India. Official apps only offer 10-12 standard languages, leaving farmers in Mewar, Malwa, or Bundelkhand unheard.

3. Severe Extension Officer Deficit

One government extension officer serves 1,100+ farm families. Farmers wait 4-7 days for an expert visit while foliar pathogens devastate their yield.
